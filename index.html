02 / About

About me

My background is in maritime and warehouse operations. I now apply data analysis to inventory monitoring in my day-to-day work.

After working in cruise operations, I moved into warehouse administration at PT Espera Satya Offset, where I work with inventory data.

Alongside my work, I'm pursuing a bachelor's degree in Data Science at Universitas Insan Cita Indonesia. I've also studied data analytics through Pacmann and Digital Skola, and completed a Data Analyst internship with Elevvo Pathways.

My projects and training cover Excel, SQL, Python, and data visualization, including sales dashboards and exploratory data analysis.

03 / Approach

Analysis process

The main steps in an analysis, from defining the problem to presenting recommendations.

  1. 01

    Define.

    Define the business problem, the intended users, and the questions the analysis needs to answer.

  2. 02

    Prepare.

    Clean and validate the data. Document its sources, limitations, and any assumptions.

  3. 03

    Analyze.

    Explore patterns, test assumptions, and create visualizations to explain the findings.

  4. 04

    Recommend.

    Present the findings, explain their limitations, and suggest next steps.
