03 / Approach

The question
comes first.

A framework for moving from raw data to a useful recommendation.

  1. 01

    Define.

    Start with the decision. Understand the problem, the audience, and what a useful answer looks like.

  2. 02

    Prepare.

    Get the foundations right. Clean the data, check its quality, and document the assumptions.

  3. 03

    Analyze.

    Follow the evidence. Explore patterns, test assumptions, and choose visuals that make the findings clear.

  4. 04

    Recommend.

    Make the work useful. Explain the findings, acknowledge limitations, and identify the next step.
