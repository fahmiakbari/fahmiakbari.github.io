How I work with data

  1. 01

    Understand the problem

    Start with the business question, who needs the answer, and the decision it will inform.

  2. 02

    Check the data

    Check completeness, consistency, and calculations. Document assumptions and limitations before analyzing.

  3. 03

    Investigate the patterns

    Compare trends and segments, investigate unusual results, and test possible explanations.

  4. 04

    Recommend next steps

    Explain the findings, suggest practical actions, and define what to monitor next.
