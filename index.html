03 / Approach

How I work with data

I start with the decision the analysis needs to support, then work through the data to produce findings that are clear, traceable, and useful.

  1. 01

    Understand the business question

    Clarify the problem, who will use the analysis, and what decision they need to make. Agree on the scope and metric definitions so the work answers a specific business question.

  2. 02

    Establish reliable data

    Check for missing records, duplicates, inconsistent formats, and unexpected values. Validate calculations against the source and document assumptions so the findings can be trusted.

  3. 03

    Investigate what drives performance

    Compare trends, segments, and exceptions to understand where performance differs and what needs attention. Test possible explanations and distinguish what the data shows from what still needs investigation.

  4. 04

    Make the findings useful

    Present the key findings through clear reports or dashboards, explain their business implications, and recommend practical next steps. Identify what to monitor afterward to assess whether those actions are working.
